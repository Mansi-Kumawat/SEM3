## 🔑 KEY DEFINITIONS (RAT LO)

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Social Spam** | Unsolicited, malicious content on social media for exploitation |
| **Phishing** | Fake emails/websites to steal credentials |
| **Folksonomy** | User-generated classification (bottom-up, free vocabulary) |
| **Geosocial Mining** | Location-tagged data analysis for spatial patterns |
