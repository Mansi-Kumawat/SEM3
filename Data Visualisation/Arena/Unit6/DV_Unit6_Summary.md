# DV Unit 6 — Quick Summary
## Module 6: Density & Overplotting

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

1. **Overplotting**: Too many overlapping points, hides patterns
2. **2D Histogram**: Bins data in 2D grid, shows density
3. **Contour Lines**: Connect equal values, show density/elevation
4. **Redundant Coding**: Multiple visual cues for same info
