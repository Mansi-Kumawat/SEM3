## Answer Key (Selected)

### Section A:
- **A1:** Turning unstructured text into visual insights that reveal patterns, themes, and sentiment.
- **A2:** Collect, Clean, Tokenise, Normalise, Count, Visualise
- **A3:** Frequency — larger words appear more often
- **A4:** Terms as nodes, shared appearances as links — reveals relationships
- **A5:** Root word branching into phrases that follow — shows context
- **A6:** Cosine, Jaccard, Euclidean (any three)
- **A7:** Data, Visuals, Narrative
- **A8:** Multiple data elements in same visual space for direct comparison
- **A9:** Equal ratio (multiplicative change)
- **A10:** Area (not radius)
